1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

VelocIQ is an enterprise-grade connected vehicle telematics and intelligent fleet management platform designed to solve the fundamental physical contradiction of ground fleet transit: the non-linear penalty of speed on fuel and energy economy.

Traditional fleet telematics systems track vehicle coordinates and scalar odometer readings, failing to understand the thermodynamic and aerodynamic physics governing energy consumption. VelocIQ unifies real-time ECU sensor streaming, aerodynamic drag modeling, GLOSA traffic signal speed advisories, living digital twin behavioral learning, and an interactive 3D WebGL engine simulation into a single mission-control dashboard.

3. Mathematical & Physics Modeling Engine

All intelligence in VelocIQ is anchored in continuous mathematical equations implemented in src/utils/speedMileagePhysics.js and src/utils/engineTwinPhysics.js.

3.1 Aerodynamic Drag Force & Cubic Power Dissipation

Aerodynamic resistance accounts for over 50% of total energy dissipation at highway velocities:

Aerodynamic Drag Force (Newtons)
F_drag = 0.5 · ρ · Cd · A · v²
Mechanical Power Dissipated (Watts)
P_drag = F_drag · v = 0.5 · ρ · Cd · A · v³

Where air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³, Cd is the vehicle drag coefficient (Sedan: 0.28, SUV: 0.38, Hatchback: 0.32), A is the frontal area (m²), and v is relative airspeed in m/s. Accelerating from 90 km/h to 120 km/h scales power cubically by (120/90)³ = 2.37×.

3.2 Parabolic Speed vs. Mileage Curve

Internal combustion engines peak in thermal efficiency at moderate mid-range engine loads (55–65 km/h). VelocIQ calculates fuel economy M(v) in km/L using a second-order polynomial with an aerodynamic decay penalty:

Speed vs. Mileage Equation
M(v) = M_peak · [1 - α · ((v - v_sweet) / v_sweet)²] - β · (v / 100)³ - γ_payload

3.3 Kinetic Braking Energy Loss (Stop-and-Go Tax)

Every mechanical braking event converts momentum into waste heat:

Kinetic Dissipation
ΔEk = 0.5 · m · (v_initial² - v_final²)
Fuel Liters Wasted
Fuel_wasted = ΔEk / (Gasoline Energy Density 34,200 kJ/L · 28% Thermal Efficiency)

3.4 GLOSA (Green Light Optimal Speed Advisory)

Given a V2X intersection at distance D (meters) with remaining phase duration T_rem (seconds):

Advisory Target Speed
v_target = D / T_rem

If v_target ≤ speed limit, the driver coasts to pass through without decelerating to a complete halt, eliminating 100% of the stop-and-go kinetic penalty.

3.5 Thermodynamic Mean-Value Engine Model (MVEM) & Air Leak FDI

In src/utils/enginePhysicsFDI.js, the intake plenum pressure dynamics are modeled as:

Intake Manifold Pressure Differential
dP_m / dt = (R · T_m / V_m) · (m_throttle_dot + m_leak_dot - m_cyl_dot)

Orifice leak equations classify vacuum breaches: Intake Manifold Gasket Blowouts (P0171/P0106), Throttle Coupler Tears (P0101), and Exhaust Header Flange Cracks (P0131).

4. Comprehensive Operational Module Catalog

Module 1: Live Telemetry & Aerodynamics (/dashboard)

Dual circular high-refresh SVG gauges (Speed & RPM), live parabolic speed-mileage radar, instantaneous aerodynamic drag calculation, OBD-II DTC diagnostic scanner, and BLE connection resilience.

Module 2: GPS Expressway Navigation & GLOSA (/navigation)

Real-road navigation across the 25 km Delhi Fleet Corridor (Connaught Place to IGI Airport Cargo Terminal). Features rotating directional vehicle glyphs, turn-by-turn HUD, 4-tier tile provider switcher (Esri Dark Canvas, OpenStreetMap, Esri Satellite, TomTom), GLOSA intersection speed countdowns, Limp-Home Range Governor, Open-Meteo live weather, and persistent trip log replay.

Module 3: Speed-to-Mileage What-If Lab (/simulator)

Dedicated physics experimentation workbench. Adjust cruising speed, vehicle profile, payload weight (kg), headwind/tailwind vector (km/h), ambient temperature (°C), fuel price, and stop frequency. Visualizes dual-axis Recharts curves (Mileage vs. Range) and saves Scenarios A, B, and C for side-by-side evaluation.

Module 4: 3D Interactive WebGL Engine Twin & FDI (/engine-twin)

Three.js mechanical simulation of a 4-cylinder engine block. Piston kinematics synchronized with vehicle RPM. Features CAD exploded factor slider (0.0 to 1.0), dynamic thermal shaders (70°C to 105°C+), dynamometer power sweep curves, live cylinder pressure oscilloscopes, and simulated intake/exhaust vacuum leak fault injection.

Module 5: Predictive Maintenance & Wear Runway (/maintenance)

Real-time degradation tracking for Engine Oil, Brake Pads, Starter Battery, and Cooling System. Features AI Remaining Useful Life (RUL) countdowns (days and km remaining) and one-click technician service resets.

Module 6: Fleet Garage & Multi-Asset Tracking (/fleet)

Regional multi-vehicle map canvas tracking all assets across Delhi NCR simultaneously. Vector status pins (Active emerald pulse, Idle amber, Maintenance ruby). Roster management with driver dispatch assignment and one-click telematics monitoring switcher.

Module 7: Driver Safety Score & Kinetic Waste Log (/safety)

Algorithmic safety score (0–100) with dynamic penalties for harsh braking (-3.5 pts), rapid acceleration (-2.5 pts), speeding (-0.8 pts), and overrev (-0.5 pts). Logs kinetic energy dissipated in kJ per incident and delivers AI coaching feedback.

Module 8: Threat Defense & Remote Immobilizer (/security)

Autonomous threat radar (Secure, Elevated, Critical, Alert), fuel siphoning detection, CAN bus dropout alarm, municipal geofence boundary sentry, and remote dispatcher engine immobilizer kill-switch.

Module 9: Living Digital Twin & Self-Learning Optimizer (/digital-twin)

90-day operational baselines, multi-variate drift metrics, AI Driver Twin behavioral modeling (jerk index, throttle ramp aggressiveness), 3-pillar optimization radar (Safety, Efficiency, Health), and 6-stage closed loop self-learning cycle (Sense → Model → Diagnose → Prescribe → Actuate → Verify).

Module 10: AI Analytics & 3-Tier Financial Matrix (/analytics)

Side-by-side financial comparison between Eco (70 km/h), Cruise (95 km/h), and Rush (120 km/h) accounting for driver Value of Time ($/hr). Includes interactive neural network hyperparameter model tuner (learning rates, epochs) with live training loss vs. validation curves.

6. Visual Design System & Strict Zero-Emoji Policy

To maintain mission-critical enterprise design standards, VelocIQ operates under a Strict Zero-Emoji Policy:

  • 100% Bespoke Inline Vector SVGs: Every icon, glyph, status indicator, and warning light is a hand-crafted SVG in src/components/icons.
  • Consistent Multi-OS Rendering: Eliminates operating system emoji font discrepancies between Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile devices.
  • Dark Glassmorphism UI: Deep slate base (#020617), translucent cards with backdrop-blur-md, and neon signal accents (Cyan, Emerald, Amber, Rose).

7. Central Telemetry Data Contract

The central simulation engine in src/App.jsx broadcasts the following state object every 300ms: 

interface TelemetryPacket {
  speed: number;             // Vehicle speed in km/h (0 - 180)
  rpm: number;               // Engine RPM (800 - 6500)
  coolant: number;           // Coolant temperature in °C (60 - 115)
  maf: number;               // Mass Air Flow sensor in g/s (2.0 - 45.0)
  fuel: number;              // Fuel tank level percentage (0 - 100%)
  tripMileage: number;       // Cumulative trip fuel economy in km/L
  co2: number;               // Cumulative CO2 emitted in kg
  score: number;             // Driver safety score (0 - 100)
  voltage: number;           // 12V Starter Battery voltage (11.8 - 14.4V)
  partsWear: {               // Component health percentage
    oil: number;
    brakes: number;
    battery: number;
    coolant: number;
  };
  predictedFailureDays: {    // Days until service required
    oil: number;
    brakes: number;
    battery: number;
    coolant: number;
  };
  route: {
    progress: number;        // Percentage completed (0 - 100%)
    lat: number;
    lon: number;
    heading: number;         // Compass heading in degrees (0 - 360)
    etaMinutes: number;      // Dynamic arrival estimate
  };
}
